# How can I make money with Bayesian Inference?

Slides for a reading-group session on **TabPFN** and prior-data fitted networks (PFNs).

**[Open the slides (PDF)](TabPFN_Reading_Group.pdf)**

Topics: the posterior predictive and why it is hard to compute, how a PFN learns it from simulated data, TabPFN's causal prior, why it works, and how it performs in Hollmann et al. (2025).

## Main references

- Müller et al. (2022), "Transformers can do Bayesian inference," ICLR.
- Hollmann et al. (2023), "TabPFN: A transformer that solves small tabular classification problems in a second," ICLR.
- Hollmann et al. (2025), "Accurate predictions on small data with a tabular foundation model," *Nature* 637, 319–326.

Figures marked as such are reproduced from Hollmann et al. (2025).

Slides by Furkan Danisman, based on lecture slides by Linbo Wang and Furkan Danisman.
